# UC-0005 — Release collateral is compiled, not invented

**Personas:** Marcus, solo founder acting as his own marketing department, and the marketing
bot he points at the repo. **Job (REQ-0002 §6):** assemble release messaging with near-zero
fresh invention, in a form a technical reader can verify in minutes.

## Context

At release, the Compiler role reads `docs/collateral/` and generates Markdown packs per
feature and per release, audience-targeted (developer-facing, executive-facing) — FR-3.
The marketing bot consumes **compiled packs only**, never raw entries: one evidence-backed
surface, so a bad prompt cannot resurrect a claim the audit rejected. This is the "claims
carry receipts" promise made visible (REQ-0002 §2).

## Scenarios

**S1 [happy] A release pack assembles itself from captured entries.**
GIVEN a release with captured collateral, WHEN Marcus runs the compiler, THEN he gets a
per-release pack and per-feature packs, each claim carrying its evidence link, audience
sections separated (developer-facing, executive-facing) (FR-3).

**S2 [happy] The marketing bot reads the pack, not the repo.**
GIVEN Marcus's marketing bot needs launch copy, WHEN it is pointed at the compiled pack,
THEN every claim it may use resolves to cited evidence inside the pack, and it never has to
chase the team for quotes (REQ-0002 §2).

**S3 [edge] A broken evidence link downgrades, not disappears.**
GIVEN a pack claim whose evidence link no longer resolves (the test was renamed), WHEN the
Compiler role re-checks every evidence link while building the pack, THEN the claim is
demoted to `unverified` in the pack and the stale source entry is named for a human to fix —
the claim neither passes silently nor vanishes (AC-2; ARCH-0001 §7: the compiler is a
role, not a gate).

**S4 [failure] An unverified claim presented as fact is caught.**
GIVEN a generated pack contains a claim that neither cites evidence nor carries the
`unverified` mark, WHEN the Compiler role's self-check runs over the pack, THEN it refuses
to deliver the pack and names the offending claim — and Marcus, who must read the pack
before use, enforces the same rule as a human (ARCH-0001 §7: gates stay pure, the pack is
an artifact a human reads). The traceability discipline of code, applied to marketing (AC-2).

## Out of scope

Copywriting beyond compilation from captured entries; CMS/email/social integrations
(REQ-0002 §4); deciding *which* features to lead a release story with — the compiler
organizes, the human aims.
