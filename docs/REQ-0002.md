# REQ-0002: Value & Collateral Capture

Status: Draft
Parent: REQ-0001 (product)

## 1. Problem statement

Agents doing engineering work constantly discover marketing-relevant facts —
differentiators, pros and cons, caveats, competitive observations, demo-worthy
moments, evidence of quality. Today those observations evaporate into chat
logs. By release time the marketing story is reconstructed from memory or,
worse, invented — losing credibility with exactly the technical audience this
product targets.

## 2. Description

The system captures value signals as structured artifacts during normal role
work and compiles them into audience-targeted collateral when a feature is
integrated into the solution. Marketing — human or bot — pulls from one
evidence-backed source instead of chasing the team for quotes.

Design principle: the same traceability discipline that anchors code to
requirements anchors marketing claims to evidence. **Claims carry receipts.**
A selling point is a claim; it cites the test, benchmark, audit, or artifact
that proves it — or it is marked unverified.

## 3. Functional requirements

- FR-1: Roles record collateral entries (selling points, pros/cons, caveats,
  competitive notes, demo moments) as a side effect of normal work, tagged
  with the REQ/UC/feature IDs the observation concerns.
- FR-2: Every collateral claim cites evidence (test run, benchmark, audit
  finding, artifact link); entries without evidence are marked `unverified`.
- FR-3: A compiler role generates collateral packs per feature and per
  release, audience-targeted (developer-facing, executive-facing).
- FR-4: Collateral coverage is visible: a feature reaching integration with
  zero captured value signals is flagged.

## 4. Boundaries

**In scope:** capture during role work; evidence links; compilation to
Markdown collateral packs.

**Out of scope:** external marketing integrations (CMS, email, social);
copywriting beyond compilation from captured entries; any claim not tied to
engineering evidence.

## 5. Acceptance criteria (testable)

- AC-1: GIVEN a role discovers a differentiator or trade-off, WHEN it produces
  its work artifact, THEN a collateral entry tagged to the feature exists and
  cites evidence or is marked `unverified`.
- AC-2: GIVEN a generated collateral pack, WHEN audited, THEN every claim
  resolves to cited evidence or is explicitly marked `unverified`.
- AC-3: GIVEN a feature at integration, WHEN collateral coverage is checked,
  THEN features with zero entries are flagged.

## 6. Success measures

- At release, collateral packs are assembled from captured entries with near-
  zero fresh invention; a technical reader can verify any claim in minutes.

## 7. Open questions

- Storage shape: one `docs/collateral/` log vs per-feature collateral files
  vs entries embedded in work artifacts. (Architecture decision.)
- Does the compiler run as a role in the pipeline (gate-adjacent) or purely
  on demand?
- Does the marketing bot consume raw artifacts directly, or only compiled
  packs?
