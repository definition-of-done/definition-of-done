# UC-0004 — Selling points are captured while the work happens

**Persona:** Priya, engineer, and any agent role she supervises. **Job (REQ-0002 §1):** stop
marketing-relevant discoveries — differentiators, trade-offs, caveats, demo moments — from
evaporating into chat logs.

## Context

Capture must be a side effect of normal role work, not a chore bolted on afterwards
(FR-1). Each role's skill instructs it to append collateral entries to `docs/collateral/`
as it produces its work artifact (ARCH-0001 §4, §7): id, `req:`/`uc:`/feature tags,
evidence link, verified flag. A claim with no evidence yet is still worth keeping — it is
written down and marked `unverified` rather than dropped or dressed up (FR-2).

## Scenarios

**S1 [happy] A differentiator is recorded at the moment it is proven.**
GIVEN an implementer agent discovers its gate-checked module outperforms the common
alternative while completing its task, WHEN it produces its work artifact, THEN a collateral
entry tagged to the feature exists citing the benchmark run, and the entry is machine-
checkable (FR-1, FR-2, AC-1).

**S2 [happy] Cons and caveats are captured with the same duty as pros.**
GIVEN a Review Agent orders a refactor or an architect records a rejected alternative,
WHEN the role finishes, THEN the trade-off appears as a collateral entry with the review
notes or ADR as its evidence link — the pros/cons list is built from honest records, not
written fresh for launch (FR-1, AC-1).

**S3 [edge] An observation with no evidence yet is kept, honestly labeled.**
GIVEN a role notices something marketing-relevant it cannot currently prove (a competitive
observation), WHEN it records the entry, THEN the entry exists with `unverified` set instead
of the claim being dropped or stated as fact (FR-2).

**S4 [failure] A silent feature is flagged at integration.**
GIVEN a feature reaches integration with zero collateral entries, WHEN `dod trace` runs its
collateral-coverage check, THEN the feature is flagged — zero captured value signals is a
finding, not a pass (FR-4, AC-3; ARCH-0001 §7).

## Out of scope

Deciding which captured entries are *good marketing* (a human or the compiler's audience
templates decide emphasis); capturing anything not tied to engineering evidence
(REQ-0002 §4).
